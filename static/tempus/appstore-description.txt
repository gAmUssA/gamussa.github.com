Tempus is a world clock and time zone converter built for people who work, travel, and stay in touch across time zones.

NAME YOUR CLOCKS
Add clocks for cities or for the people you talk to. Give each one a custom name, an emoji, and a color tag so "Mum in Sydney" or "Design team, Berlin" is readable at a glance. Choose analog or digital faces, 12 or 24 hour time, and see the time difference from wherever you are.

CONVERT TIMES INSTANTLY
Pick any time in any zone and see it in all your clocks at once. Drag the time scrubber to slide through the day and watch every clock move together. Day and night indicators show who is awake.

FIND A MEETING TIME THAT WORKS
Select two or more clocks, set working hours for each, and Tempus shows the overlapping windows where everyone is available. Add the meeting to your calendar in one tap or share it as an .ics invite.

CITY DETAIL
Tap any clock for a full city view: a skyline illustration that changes with the time of day, sunrise and sunset times, and current weather conditions.

APPLE WATCH
Tempus on Apple Watch shows your clocks on your wrist, lets you convert times with the Digital Crown, and offers complications for your favorite clocks. Clocks sync instantly between iPhone and Apple Watch.

WIDGETS AND SYNC
Home Screen widgets keep your clocks in view. Your clocks sync across all your devices with iCloud.

PRIVACY FIRST
Tempus has no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your clocks are stored on your device and in your private iCloud database. Weather is provided by Apple Weather for the cities you add, never for your own location.
