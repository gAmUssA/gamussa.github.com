**Time My Talk** (aka *Agenda Defender*) is a dead-simple timer for talks, workshops, and meetings. Drop in an agenda with timestamps, click GO, and it shows you where you should be right now — so you stop running over on slide 3 and crashing through the Q&A.

Inspired by [Dylan Beattie's blog](https://www.dylanbeattie.net/).
