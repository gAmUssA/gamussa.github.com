How it works

Three taps from awake to asleep. One buzz back.

  1. 1

    Pick a length

    Four presets, each with its own colour. Tap one and the nap starts. You can also start a nap from Siri or a Shortcut.

  2. 2

    Close your eyes

    The ring shows time left and when you'll wake. Add or take away five minutes, or stop, without reaching for your phone.

  3. 3

    Wake to a summary

    Haptics and a tone on your wrist. Then how long you slept, your average heart rate and roughly how long you took to settle.

Smart wake

Your time is the deadline. Stirring is the trigger.

In the last few minutes Koima watches for the wrist movement that comes with surfacing. Move, and it wakes you then, while you're already coming up. Stay still, and it wakes you exactly when you asked.

Honest about the limits: Koima doesn't detect sleep stages. On a 10 or 20 minute nap most people never reach deep sleep, so waking on your first stir is a gentler finish, not a rescue. Longer naps give the timing more to work with.

Wake guarantee

Choose how sure you want to be.

Pick one in Settings on the watch. Each has conditions, spelled out on the support page. None of them is a promise.

Standard Default

A smart-alarm session plays haptics and a tone on your wrist, backed by a time-sensitive notification. A Focus that blocks time-sensitive alerts, or notifications switched off, can silence it.

Sport Mode

A Mind & Body workout keeps Koima running for the whole nap, so the wake lands on time even when watchOS would pause the app. It counts toward your Activity rings and saves no workout.

Phone Alarm

Your paired iPhone rings a backup alarm 45 seconds after your wake time, unless the watch already woke you or you stopped the nap. Only the alarm's ID and fire time go to your phone.

During a nap, a short orange line above the ring names any known problem with your choice, like "Alarm blocked" or "Focus may block". A blank line means the watch did its part, not that your iPhone armed anything.

After the nap

One question. Kept to yourself.

Koima asks once how you feel: better, the same or worse. Your answer stays on the watch with that nap. It's never sent anywhere, never trains anything, and never changes how Koima wakes you.

  • Heart rate from Apple Health during the nap, with your permission
  • One sleep sample written to Health when the nap finishes
  • Nap history on iPhone, showing only naps Koima recorded
  • Everything still works without Health access

FAQ

Questions

How does the smart wake decide when to wake me?

For the last few minutes before your chosen time, Koima watches wrist motion. Sustained movement reads as you starting to surface, and it wakes you then. If you stay still, it wakes you at the time you picked. The smart wake can only move your alarm earlier, never later.

Koima doesn't detect sleep stages and isn't a sleep tracker. On a short nap most people never reach deep sleep, so waking on your first stir is a gentler finish, not a rescue from grogginess.

Will it wake me if my watch is in Sleep Focus or on Silent?

Not always. Koima asks for time-sensitive delivery, which can reach you through some Focus modes, but a Focus that denies time-sensitive alerts, or notifications switched off, can suppress the alarm. Check Settings › Notifications › Koima on your watch and read the support page before relying on it for anything that matters.

Do I need my iPhone nearby?

Not for a normal nap. The watch app runs on its own. The optional iPhone backup alarm needs your paired iPhone in range.

Which wake guarantee should I choose?

Standard if your watch notifications and Focus allow time-sensitive alerts. Sport Mode if you nap with the watch alone and want Koima kept running for the whole nap. Phone Alarm if your iPhone is usually within reach and you want it to ring as a backup. Pick in Settings › Wake guarantee on the watch; the choice applies to your next nap.

What is the orange line above the ring?

A status line that appears only when the watch knows something is wrong with your wake guarantee: notifications off, the Sport Mode workout refused, Koima missing on iPhone, a Phone Alarm still queued, or a Focus that may block the alarm. Blank means the watch has done its part; it can't see whether your iPhone actually armed its alarm.

What does Koima do with my health data?

It reads heart rate from Apple Health during the nap and writes a sleep sample when the nap completes. Both need your permission, both stay on your device and in Apple Health, and neither is shared, sold or used for advertising. Without Health access the timer and smart wake work the same.

What happens to my answer on the “how do you feel” card?

It's stored on the watch with that nap and nowhere else. Koima doesn't send it anywhere, doesn't use it to train anything, and doesn't change how it wakes you.

How much does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Free covers every feature for three nap starts in any seven days. Koima Unlimited removes the limit for $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year. Cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone; the current period runs to its end. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.
